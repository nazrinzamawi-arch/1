THE BRIEF

HOW TO PLAY

Each design question appears above four moving target cards. Aim and select the option you believe is the best design decision.

01 · AIM

Move your pointer or tap a target. On a keyboard, use ← → to change selection.

02 · FIRE

Click or tap a target, or press Enter/Space to submit your selected answer.

03 · LEARN

Read the explanation. If you miss, you can try the question again before moving on.

04 · WIN

Earn 10 points for each correct first attempt, or 5 points after a retry. There is no timer or speed bonus.

Targets drift gently, but there is no countdown. You can also switch on reduced motion at any time. Your final results report distinguishes understanding from how fast you aim.
